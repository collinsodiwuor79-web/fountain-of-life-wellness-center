About Our Professional Care

Fountain of Life Wellness Center stands as a premier rehabilitation facility dedicated to restoring dignity and health. Our clinical team utilizes evidence-based psychological interventions to support long-term sobriety.


We provide a serene environment away from the stresses of urban life, allowing residents to focus entirely on their recovery journey through professional guidance and peer support.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality, and evidence-based rehabilitation services that empower individuals to reclaim their lives from addiction through comprehensive professional care.

Our Vision

To be the leading wellness sanctuary in the region, recognized for excellence in therapeutic innovation and successful community reintegration.

Holistic Rehabilitation Services

Inpatient Programs

Psychological Counseling

Individualized therapy sessions targeting the root causes of addiction and mental health challenges.

Wellness Activities

Integrating nutrition, mindfulness, and physical fitness into the recovery protocol.

Sustainable Recovery
