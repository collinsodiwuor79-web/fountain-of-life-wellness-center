ABOUT US

Fountain of Life is a dedicated mental health and rehabilitation referral firm based in Kisumu, committed to bridging the gap between individuals in need and professional clinical care. We specialize in providing a supportive pathway for those navigating the complexities of substance use, psychological distress, and behavioral health challanges. By establishing strong collaborative partnership with leading private hospitals and specialized medical facilities, we ensure that every client receives a personalized recovery roadmap. Our mission is to facilitate seamless access to high-quality inpatient and outpatient services, ensuring that the journey towrds wellness is grounded in dignity, professional integrity, and comprehensive support.Core services are: Clinical Assessments & Referrals.
